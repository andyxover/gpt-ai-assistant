Grade 7 Science — Semester 1 Syllabus
Teacher: Ms. Lin
Sections: 7A, 7B, 7C
Textbook: TCS Integrated Science, Book 1

[Course Goals]
Build foundational understanding of scientific inquiry, living things,
and matter — preparing students for mechanics and chemistry next semester.

[Weekly Schedule]
Week 1: Lab safety, basic measurement and SI units
Week 2: Scientific notation, error and significant figures
Week 3: Common features of life, biotic vs abiotic
Week 4: Classification hierarchy (Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species)
Week 5: Cell structure and function (membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, organelles)
Week 6: Cell division and growth, unicellular vs multicellular
Week 7: States of matter, physical and chemical change
Week 8: Solutions, solute and solvent, percent concentration (w/w)
Week 9: First Mid-Term Exam (covering Ch.1 - Ch.2)

[Assessment]
Quizzes 30% · Lab reports 20% · Mid-term exam 50%

[Notes]
The cell unit is the most important content of the semester — students
should especially focus on comparing animal vs. plant cells.
Mid-term format: MCQ 60%, fill-in-the-blank 20%, short answer 20%.
